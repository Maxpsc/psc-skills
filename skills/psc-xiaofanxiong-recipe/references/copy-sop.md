# Copy SOP

## Deliverables

For each dish, generate:

- 4 to 5 title options
- 3 opening-copy options
- 2 four-line rhyme options
- 1 hashtag block

## Title Rules

- Keep each title within 20 Chinese characters.
- Make the title feel catchy and suitable for Xiaohongshu.
- Keep "小饭熊" present in most options.
- Prefer appetite, comfort, ease, healing, or home-cooking hooks.
- Avoid titles that sound like pure ads or generic SEO phrases.

## Opening Copy Rules

- Write 3 distinct openings.
- Keep the tone warm, vivid, and conversational.
- Reference the dish's taste, weather, mood, or mealtime scene.
- Make "小饭熊" feel like an active character cooking for the reader.

## Four-Line Rhyme Rules

- Write 2 versions.
- Keep each version to 4 short lines.
- Make it easy to read aloud.
- One version can lean warm and healing.
- One version can lean playful and energetic.

## Hashtag Rules

- Output one line or one block of hashtags.
- Use `#` prefixes.
- Mix broad food tags with dish-specific tags and series tags.
- Always consider including tags related to `小饭熊的厨房`, home cooking, easy recipes, and the dish itself.
- Prefer a warm, living-food-account style similar to:
  `#养生鸡汤 #小饭熊的厨房 #滋补汤 #煲汤 #暖心暖胃 #一学就会 #跟着小饭熊学做菜 #菌菇鸡汤 #换季养生 #家常汤 #喝汤 #治愈系美食 #一只会做饭的熊`
- Reuse the structure of that example while swapping in dish-relevant tags, and keep `#小饭熊的厨房`, `#跟着小饭熊学做菜`, and `#一只会做饭的熊` as strong default candidates.

## Voice

Keep the voice:

- warm
- approachable
- lightly playful
- home-cooking oriented
- suitable for Xiaohongshu readers

Avoid:

- stiff copywriting tone
- hard-sell marketing language
- overdone internet slang
