# Image SOP

## Purpose

Use this reference to build the internal 3-page image-generation instructions for a "小饭熊的厨房" Xiaohongshu recipe note. The final image deliverable should be the actual downloaded images produced through the browser workflow, not the prompt text itself.

## Global Style Rules

Apply these rules to every page:

- Use a warm mixed-media scrapbook look.
- Keep the page vertical and suitable for Xiaohongshu graphic posts.
- Put a fixed banner at top-left: `小饭熊的厨房`.
- Make the banner look like yellow or milk-coffee torn tape.
- Put a "小饭熊" chef sticker at top-right.
- Show the difficulty label under the sticker.
- Add a small amount of hand-drawn elements such as hearts, stars, arrows, steam, ingredient doodles, tape, labels, and torn-paper edges.
- Use a stable palette built from milk coffee, orange-yellow, kraft paper, and warm brown, then blend in the dish's own main color.

Avoid:

- modern minimalism
- cold palettes
- glossy commercial-ad styling
- messy text layout
- decorations that reduce readability

## Page 1 Cover

Must include:

- large handwritten Chinese title: `菜品名称`
- one short Chinese selling-line subtitle
- one English subtitle: `英文菜名`
- a large hero dish photo occupying at least half of the page
- a left memo card titled `好吃的秘诀` or `味道特点`
- 3 to 4 short selling bullets on that memo card
- a floating vertical time card with prep time, cook time, and servings
- a bottom-left ingredient area titled `主要食材`
- a bottom-right seasoning area titled `基础调味`
- one warm closing slogan near the bottom

Food photography direction:

- realistic and appetizing
- bright color and visible gloss
- light steam when appropriate
- simple ceramic bowl or plate
- wood table, bamboo mat, or home-kitchen background

## Page 2 Process

Must include:

- main title: `菜品名称怎么做`
- one subtitle describing the cooking rhythm or method
- a central process area taking at least two-thirds of the page
- 4 to 6 step cards
- each card should include step number, short instruction text, and a stage image
- a left bottom area titled `小饭熊提醒` with 2 to 3 reminders
- a right bottom area titled `成功关键` with 2 to 3 key tips

The page should feel operational and clear. The user should understand the recipe at a glance.

## Page 3 Nutrition And Variations

Main title:

- `营养特点 & 衍生菜品`

Must include:

- a nutrition card covering key nutrients and approximate calories
- a daily pairing card
- 3 to 4 derivative dish cards
- each derivative card should include a title, a short description, and a small dish image
- one warm closing slogan near the bottom

This page should feel practical and informative without becoming medical or overly technical.

## Prompt Assembly Notes

When writing the final prompt for a page:

- name the page goal first
- describe the full composition next
- specify the fixed series anchors
- specify the food-photo texture and appetite cues
- end with explicit negatives such as no cold minimalism, no ad-poster look, no unreadable clutter
